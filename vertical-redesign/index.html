Welcome to Vertical

You'll say, "That was church?"

We exist to help people take their next step with Jesus — no matter where they're starting.

From the moment you walk in, you'll find high-energy worship, honest preaching and a room full of people who are done playing it safe with their faith. Pastor Marcus Reyes planted Vertical in 2015 with one conviction: the gospel changes everything. We think you're about to find out why.
