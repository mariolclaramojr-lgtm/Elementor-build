# Presenter Cam (Chrome extension)

Shows your camera in a small floating window so people can see you while you present in Google Meet.

## Install
1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
2. Click **Load unpacked** and pick this `camera-extension` folder.
3. Pin **Presenter Cam** from the puzzle-piece menu.

## Use
1. Click the Presenter Cam icon. A small camera window opens. Allow camera access the first time.
2. Pick your camera, and turn **Mirror** / **Circle** on or off.
3. Click **Pop out** to get an always-on-top Picture-in-Picture window. Drag it to a corner and resize it.
4. In Meet, share **your entire screen** (or a window the camera bubble sits over). Your audience then sees your camera on top of your slides.

## Notes
- Meet and this extension can use the same camera at once in Chrome.
- If you share only a single **tab**, the floating window isn't part of that tab, so viewers won't see it. Share the entire screen instead.
- The Pop out (Picture-in-Picture) window is always rectangular. The Circle option only changes the regular window.
