# Presenter Cam (Chrome extension)

Shows your camera in a small floating window so people can see you while you present in Google Meet.

## Install
1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
2. Click **Load unpacked** and pick this `camera-extension` folder.
3. Pin **Presenter Cam** from the puzzle-piece menu.

## Use

Click the Presenter Cam icon. You get two options:

### Show camera on this tab (recommended for Meet)
1. Go to the tab you want to present (for example, your Google Slides tab).
2. Click the Presenter Cam icon, then **Show camera on this tab**. Allow camera access for that site the first time.
3. A round camera bubble appears. Drag it to move it. Drag the corner arrow to resize it. Hover to see the mirror (⇋) and remove (✕) buttons.
4. In Meet, click **Present → A tab** and pick that tab. Viewers see your slides with your camera bubble on top.

The bubble stays visible when Slides goes fullscreen. Click the icon and the same button again to remove it. Reloading the page also removes it.

### Open floating window
A separate always-on-top window. It only shows up in Meet when you share your **entire screen**. If you share a single window or a single tab, it is left out.

## Notes
- Meet and this extension can use the same camera at once in Chrome.
- Chrome doesn't allow extensions on `chrome://` pages or the Chrome Web Store, so the tab bubble won't work there.
