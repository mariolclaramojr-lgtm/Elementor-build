Why We Give

Giving is an an act of worship and obedience from a heart surrendered to Christ ... a choice we make to follow God's instruction for us.

The church doesn't need anything from you. We want something for you. God certainly doesn't need anything from us, it's all His anyway. We give out of our need to act on our faith.
