Welcome home

Come as you are.

We want church to be a place where your entire family is encouraged to draw closer to Christ.

Visiting somewhere new can be intimidating. We want you to feel comfortable and welcome when you walk through our doors. Smiling faces will greet you and show you where to go. Enjoy a cup of coffee and relax with us!

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Still wondering about something? Call us at 111-123-4567 and we'll help.

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. You'll see everything from jeans to dresses on a Sunday morning, so come as you are.

What should I expect when I arrive?

Our greeters will meet you at the front doors, help you find a seat and point you to the coffee bar. Services include worship music, a short welcome and a message from the Bible.

What do you have for my kids?

Lewis Kids runs during both Sunday services for children from birth through 5th grade. Check-in is in the main lobby, and every volunteer is background-checked.

How long is a service?

About 75 minutes, start to finish. Feel free to stay afterwards to meet people and grab another cup of coffee.
