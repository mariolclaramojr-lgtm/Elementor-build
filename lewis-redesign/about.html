Our values

Experience. Connect. Respond.

01

Experience

Experience a real God for your real life. You don't have to have it all figured out before you come to Him. God loves you right where you are, and we want our church to be a place where you truly experience His love.

02

Connect

Connect in relationship with God and others. Life is meant to be lived in close community with our Creator and the ones He created, so we help you grow closer to God and to our community of believers.

03

Respond

Respond to what God is doing in your life by sharing with and serving others. We want our faith to show in the way we live, so we keep our eyes open for ways to serve.

Our beliefs

What we believe.

The gospel of Christ is the hope of the world. This belief is the foundation of our church and shapes all we preach, teach, and practice.

The Trinity

In one God who exists eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus

That Jesus is the Son of God, who took on our sins through death on a cross and rose again so that we might live eternally with God.

The Holy Spirit

That the Holy Spirit dwells in believers and empowers them to live Godly lives.

The Bible

That the Bible is the inspired word of God.

Prayer

In the power of prayer, and we encourage everyone to bring their hearts to God through prayer.

Salvation

That all people are made in the likeness of God and that salvation is available to them through Jesus.
