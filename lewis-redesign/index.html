Welcome to Lewis Church

More than a place to go. A family to belong to.

We're a community of people who have been changed by the love of God, and we want to change our community in response.

Following the model of Jesus, it's the heart of our church to love those around us by meeting needs, sharing our lives, and offering the life-changing grace of the Christian faith.
