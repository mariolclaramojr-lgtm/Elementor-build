About Us

Christ Son of the Living God Church

Christ Son of the Living God Church is more than a church. We are a Christ-centered community passionate about seeing lives changed and God’s Kingdom established across the world.

Our vision for world conquest drives us to raise leaders, reach the lost, and love unconditionally. We’re here to disciple nations, serve our city, and build a legacy of faith that impacts generations.

What We Do

Our Path to Transformation

At CSLG Church, we believe everyone has a purpose. We follow four clear steps to help people grow, just like Jesus did. From knowing Him for the first time to becoming strong, confident leaders, we’re here to walk with you every step of the way.

01

Win

We share the Gospel with love and boldness, bringing people to a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

02

Consolidate

We guide new believers through their early steps of faith, helping them build strong spiritual foundations.

03

Disciple

We equip every believer with the Word of God, training them to live in purpose and truth.

04

Send

We release empowered disciples into ministry—locally and globally—to reach others and advance God’s Kingdom.
