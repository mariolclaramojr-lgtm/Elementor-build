MISSIONS
Go therefore and make disciples of all nations
Matthew 28:19
At Stewartstown Baptist Church, missions are at the heart of our calling. We believe that sharing the gospel and serving others, both near and far, is a vital part of fulfilling the Great Commission. Through local, national, and international mission efforts, we strive to be the hands and feet of Christ, meeting both the physical and spiritual needs of those around us. Whether through disaster relief, outreach programs, or mission trips, we are committed to making a difference in the world by spreading God’s love and sharing the message of hope found in Jesus Christ. 
Join the mission
Serve the Kingdom on upcoming mission trips
We invite you to be a part of our mission efforts and experience the joy of serving others in Jesus’ name. There are many ways you can get involved—whether it’s through giving, praying, or directly participating in mission trips and outreach projects. By engaging in missions, you’ll not only impact the lives of others but also grow in your own faith as you witness firsthand how God is at work in the world. Join us as we take the gospel to new places, showing God’s love in action and making an eternal impact.
