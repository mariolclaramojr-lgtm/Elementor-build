MEN
Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong.
1 Corinthians 16:13
Our Men’s Ministry is dedicated to equipping and encouraging men to grow in their faith, lead their families well, and serve their community for the glory of God. Through Bible studies, fellowship events, and service opportunities, we seek to build strong, Christ-centered relationships that challenge and inspire. Whether through discipleship, accountability, or simply sharing life together, we desire to see men step into their God-given roles with confidence and purpose. No matter where you are in your spiritual journey, there is a place for you to connect, grow, and make a lasting impact. 
Small Groups
Get Connected. Go Deeper.
Men’s Small Groups provide a space for deeper connection, personal growth, and spiritual accountability. These groups meet regularly to study God’s Word, pray together, and support one another through life’s challenges. In a world that often isolates men, our small groups offer a community where honesty, encouragement, and brotherhood thrive. Whether you’re looking for guidance, friendship, or a place to strengthen your faith, Men’s Small Groups are designed to help you grow as a follower of Christ and lead with integrity in every area of life.
