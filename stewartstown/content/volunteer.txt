VOLUNTEER
For even the Son of Man came not to be served, but to serve...
Mark 10:45
Volunteering at Stewartstown Baptist Church is a meaningful way to serve God and make a difference in the lives of others. Whether you’re helping in children’s ministry, greeting guests, leading a small group, or assisting with special events, every role is an opportunity to contribute to the church’s mission of sharing the love of Christ. Our volunteers are the heart and hands of our church, and we believe that serving others is not only a privilege but also a way to grow spiritually. If you’re passionate about using your gifts to impact others, we invite you to join our team and become an integral part of the work God is doing here at Stewartstown Baptist Church. 
Serve in our church
Use your unique gifts to help build the Kingdom.
At Stewartstown Baptist Church, we believe that each person is uniquely gifted by God, and those gifts are meant to be used to serve others. Volunteering is a powerful way to put your talents, skills, and passions to work for God’s kingdom. Whether you have a heart for teaching, organizing, greeting, or serving behind the scenes, there is a place for you to make a meaningful impact. When you use your gifts to serve, not only do you bless others, but you also grow in your own faith and deepen your connection to the church family. We encourage you to discover how God has uniquely equipped you and step into a volunteer role that aligns with your strengths and passions.
