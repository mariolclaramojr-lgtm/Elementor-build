GIVING
For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.
- Matthew 6:21
Why we give.
God is generous and so he calls us to be as well.  What we do with what God has given us shows the world where our hearts are at and helps proclaim the gospel. We want to glorify God with every area of our lives, and that includes what we do with our finances.
Ways To Give
Give Online
Giving online is a simple, secure, and convenient way to support the ministry and mission of our church. Whether you want to give a one-time gift or set up a recurring donation, our online giving platform makes it easy to worship through generosity from anywhere, at any time. Just click the button below to get started. Thank you for helping us share the love of Christ in our community and beyond. 
Give In Person
We’d love to see you in person! During our services, we provide an opportunity to give as part of our worship. You can place your gift in the offering plate. Envelopes are available if you'd like to designate your gift. Thank you for partnering with us in faith and generosity. 
Mail A Check
Prefer to give by mail? You’re welcome to send a check to our church office. Please make checks payable to Stewartstown Baptist Church, and mail them to the address below. Be sure to include any specific designations in the memo line. We are grateful for your continued support and faithfulness.
18631 Five Forks Road, Stewartstown, PA 17363
