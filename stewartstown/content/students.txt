STUDENTS
Growing together.
Whoever you are, wherever you're from, you are welcome here.
Our Student Ministry is a place where middle and high school students can grow in their faith, build lasting friendships, and discover God’s purpose for their lives. Through engaging Bible studies, dynamic worship, and meaningful discussions, we challenge students to deepen their relationship with Christ and live out their faith boldly. We also provide opportunities for service projects, retreats, and fun events that help students connect with each other and with God. No matter where they are in their spiritual journey, we want every student to know they are loved, valued, and called to make a difference for God’s glory.

Small Groups
Get Connected. Go Deeper.
Student Small Groups provide a space for 7th-12th graders to grow in their faith while building authentic relationships with their peers and mentors. These groups offer a relaxed and supportive environment where students can ask questions, dive deeper into Scripture, and apply biblical truths to their everyday lives. Through meaningful discussions, prayer, and fellowship, our goal is to help students develop a strong, personal faith that lasts beyond their teenage years. Whether they’re exploring their faith or looking to grow deeper, our Small Groups are a place where every student is known, encouraged, and challenged to follow Christ wholeheartedly. 
A Message from the Leaders:
Welcome!
Our names are Clay and Chloe LePore. We have been married since September of 2019 and we have three beautiful children.

As teenagers we attended this same youth group. It had such a vast impact on our lives; we look back fondly on some of the conversations, lessons, games, and events from that time. We also understand how difficult it is to transition from childhood to adulthood as Christians in the current culture. We long to make that transition easier for our students by providing them with guidance, knowledge, wisdom, practice, and encouragement.

Our students ministry has two primary goals: preaching the gospel of Christ to the lost, and helping our growing believers become mature disciples. We work with the parents, as well as the church at large, to encourage and build-up our students. By the time they graduate, we hope to see mature disciples heading out into the world, prepared for whatever they might face. We want to see our students flourish in their futures and in their walk with Christ.
