Welcome to The Gathering

We say what nobody else would

Brooklyn deserves a church that actually looks and sounds like Brooklyn.

That was the conviction Pastor Deon Alexander planted The Gathering on in 2017. Worship that crosses cultures. Preaching that doesn't flinch. A room where someone always walks out saying, "I've never heard it said like that before — and I needed it."
